Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Core Privacy Guarantee: ReconX is built on a 100% offline, local-first architecture. Your bank statements, financial data, and passwords NEVER leave your computer and are never uploaded to any cloud server.

1. Information We Collect

Because ReconX runs locally on your Windows machine, our data collection is strictly limited to license management and order fulfillment:

  • Licensing Data: A non-reversible cryptographic Machine ID (derived from hardware hashes) to validate and unlock your software license.
  • Payment & Order Data: When purchasing a license via our verified payment gateway (Razorpay), Razorpay collects payment details (UPI ID, card information, name, email/phone). We only store your order ID, payment status, and license duration.
  • Zero Financial Content: We do NOT collect, view, store, or transmit any bank statement PDFs, transaction details, account balances, or Excel files generated by the application.

2. How We Use Information

The minimal information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Generating and validating cryptographic license keys bound to your Machine ID.
  • Managing license renewals, upgrades, and anti-fraud verification.
  • Providing customer support and software updates.

3. Data Security & Storage

All client communications with our licensing server use end-to-end HTTPS/TLS encryption. License tokens are cryptographically signed using asymmetric Ed25519 cryptography. We implement strict industry-standard access controls to safeguard all license records.

4. Third-Party Services

We partner with trusted service providers to operate our services:

  • Payment Processor: Razorpay Software Private Limited processes online payments in compliance with PCI-DSS and RBI standards.
  • Hosting & Database: Vercel and Supabase provide secure cloud infrastructure for license verification APIs.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion or review of your registered license details and machine nickname at any time by contacting our support team.
