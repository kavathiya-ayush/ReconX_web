Privacy Policy & Data Security

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

100% Offline Architecture Guarantee: ReconX operates entirely on a local-first engine. When you convert PDF bank statements, all parsing, password decryption, and Excel file generation happen entirely on your computer. No banking records, passwords, or transactional data ever leave your machine.

1. Information We Collect

Because ReconX is an offline desktop tool, our data collection is strictly restricted to license validation and billing records:

  • Machine Auth ID: An anonymous hardware hash generated on your local PC to authenticate your active license token.
  • Order & Transaction Records: When you purchase a license through Razorpay, payment details (UPI ID, invoice reference) are securely processed by Razorpay. We only store the resulting Order ID, activation timestamp, and license expiration date.
  • Zero Financial Content: We do NOT collect, inspect, transmit, or back up any client PDF statements, account numbers, balances, or exported XLSX/CSV files.

2. How We Use Information

The minimal licensing data collected is used solely for:

  • Issuing cryptographically signed license keys tied to your device.
  • Managing license renewals, validity extensions, and device transfers.
  • Providing prompt customer support and bug resolutions.

3. Data Protection & Security

All communication with our licensing verification service is protected using end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption. Device license tokens use asymmetric cryptographic signatures to prevent tampering. We follow strict international data protection standards to safeguard user accounts.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work exclusively with certified, compliant infrastructure providers:

  • Payment Gateway: Razorpay Software Private Limited (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified, RBI compliant).
  • Hosting Infrastructure: Vercel Inc. and Supabase Inc. provide encrypted, secure hosting for website and license APIs.

5. Your Data Protection Rights

You have full rights to query, inspect, or request the deletion of your registered license records. Contact our privacy team anytime for support.
