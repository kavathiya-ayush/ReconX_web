Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about converting bank statements offline with ReconX.

How do I convert a password-protected bank statement PDF to Excel?

ReconX natively handles password-protected PDFs. Simply drag and drop your statement into ReconX, enter the PDF password (such as your Date of Birth or PAN), and ReconX automatically decrypts and extracts each transaction into a clean Excel spreadsheet locally.

Is ReconX 100% offline and secure for client financial data?

Yes. Unlike online cloud converters where confidential bank statements are uploaded to external servers, ReconX runs 100% locally on your Windows PC. No financial data ever leaves your computer.

Which Indian banks are supported by ReconX?

ReconX supports statements from all leading Indian banks, including HDFC Bank, State Bank of India (SBI), ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra, Bank of Baroda, Punjab National Bank (PNB), Canara Bank, IndusInd, and Yes Bank.

Can I import the converted Excel files into Tally, Zoho Books, or Busy?

Yes. ReconX formats every transaction with separated columns (Date, Particulars / Narration, Cheque / Ref No., Withdrawals, Deposits, and Running Balance) for immediate import into Tally Prime, Zoho Books, Busy, and Excel accounting workflows.

How do I install ReconX on Windows?

Click the download button to get ReconX_Setup.exe. Double-click the file to install. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click 'More info' and then 'Run anyway' to complete setup in under 30 seconds.
